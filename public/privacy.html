2. Information We Process

To provide responsive, state-of-the-art multimodal interactions, HK Samrat AI processes the following inputs strictly at your instruction:

  • User Prompts & Instructions: The text queries, questions, or code you enter are processed in real-time to generate intelligent responses.
  • Uploaded Images & Documents: Visual files (JPEG, PNG, WEBP, PDF) you attach for OCR text extraction, visual reasoning, or image generation are processed in memory and never used for non-consensual profiling.
  • Voice & Speech Synthesis: Voice inputs are transcribed locally via standard browser speech interfaces, ensuring private, hands-free conversational operation without permanent audio tracking.
  • Anonymous Diagnostic Metrics: Minimal performance telemetry (such as token latency and response times) is monitored internally to maintain fast service.
