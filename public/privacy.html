Privacy Policy for HK Samrat AI

Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to HK Samrat AI ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring your personal information is protected when you use our AI intelligence, chat, voice, and generation services.

1. Information We Process

  • User Prompts & Messages: Text and inputs you submit to generate responses, logical deductions, or creative queries.
  • Voice & Speech Audio: Audio inputs processed locally in-browser/device for voice recognition, without recording or archiving personal speech.
  • Uploaded Images & Documents: Visual media attached to provide computer vision analysis. These are processed temporarily to provide immediate answers.
  • Technical Information: Basic anonymous device, network status, or error logs to maintain system stability and high performance.

2. How We Use Information

  • To generate fast, accurate, and context-aware AI responses.
  • To provide live web-grounded search results for real-time information.
  • To improve application reliability, security, and user experience.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal conversations with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Privacy

Your chat messages, history, and generated assets are stored locally within your device's browser storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage) unless explicitly saved or shared by you. You can clear your chat history anytime directly within the App's settings.

4. Third-Party Intelligence Providers

HK Samrat AI leverages secure API architectures (including Google Gemini API services) to generate intelligent text, reasoning, and visual assets. Data handled through these APIs complies with stringent enterprise security standards.

5. Children's Privacy

HK Samrat AI is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

6. Security

We employ modern encryption protocols (HTTPS / SSL) and industry-standard security measures to protect data in transit between your device and our processing endpoints.
